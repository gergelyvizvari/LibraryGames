# Animal sound credits

Each clip is a short excerpt of a Wikimedia Commons recording: trimmed to one call, mixed to mono,
high-passed (and low-passed at 7 kHz where noted), faded at both ends, level-matched and encoded as
40 kbps MP3 (22.05 kHz). Some clips are sped up slightly (pitch kept) so every call fits in about a second.
